5. INTERNATIONAL INVESTOR ARRANGEMENT — TEMPORARY MANAGER & CR AMENDMENT

This Agreement's Package Fee is calculated for a Client who, as of the Effective Date, is outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and does not hold an Iqama. Because such an investor cannot act as the company's manager of record without Saudi residency, Invesecurity arranges a temporary Saudi-resident manager as part of the Services, on the following basis:

•A KSA resident (a Saudi national or an Iqama-holding foreign resident), acting under a Power of Attorney granted to Invesecurity, is appointed as temporary manager on the company's official records at the outset.

•The temporary manager applies for an investor visa in the Client's name.

•The Client travels to the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and obtains an Iqama, becoming a Saudi resident.

•Invesecurity then files a Commercial Registration (CR) Amendment removing the temporary manager and appointing the Client as the company's permanent manager.

Costs & Client Control

•The cost of this arrangement — a CR Amendment fee of 3,500 SAR plus a Qiwa re-subscription fee of 1,080 SAR for the new permanent manager, totalling 4,580 SAR — is included within the Package Fee stated in Section 3 and is not billed separately.

•The temporary manager holds the role in name only. MISA login credentials, one-time passwords (OTP), and bank account credentials remain under the Client's control at all times.

•Where a Saudi national is engaged to facilitate this arrangement, up to five (5) visas may be issued initially; the temporary Saudi hire cost is 1,500 SAR, plus a government fee of 2,000 SAR per visa, with each arrangement made through a verified Saudi legal structure.
